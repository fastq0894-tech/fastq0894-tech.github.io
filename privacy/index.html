Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Tawlah 777 (طاولة) is published on the App Store by the developer named on its App Store page (“we”).

Contact: fastelkoo@gmail.com

1. Who we are

Tawlah is a tile game you can play online with other people or against computer players. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect and why

a) Your account (Sign in with Apple)

  • A unique Apple identifier for your account, and the email address Apple gives us (this may be Apple’s private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”). Your name from Apple, if Apple provides it.
  • Why: to create your account and sign you in. We do not receive your Apple password.
  • Guests: if you play without signing in, you get a temporary anonymous session (about 24 hours). It has no name, email, coins or history.

b) Your profile

  • The username you choose, your portrait choice, your level, experience points (XP), and your win count.
  • Your username, portrait, level and win count are visible to other players (for example at the table, in the lobby, in friends lists and on results screens).

c) Game and coin data

  • Your coin balance and a record of every coin change (starting coins, daily bonus, stakes, prizes, hints, purchases, refunds) so balances are correct and can be checked.
  • Match history: who played, the result, scores, stakes and prizes, and when.
  • Tournaments: your entries, each tournament game’s result and points, and your place and prize. Your username, portrait, level, wins and points are shown to other players on the tournament leaderboards.
  • Coins are play money. They have no cash value and cannot be cashed out.

d) Social features

  • Friends and friend requests, room invitations, the players you block, and reports you make about other players.

e) Chat and emotes

  • Messages and emotes you send in a game room are shown to the other players in that room. Messages are checked by a profanity filter.
  • To keep players safe we keep a log of chat and emote lines for 14 days, then it is deleted automatically. Moderators can read it only to review a report.

f) Safety and moderation records

  • Reports about you or by you, warnings, chat mutes, and bans.
  • A moderation audit log records what a moderator did (for example “muted 24 hours”) together with the player’s username at that time. This log is kept for accountability, even after an account is deleted.
  • If an account is banned for serious abuse, we keep a one-way hash (a scrambled fingerprint that cannot be turned back into your Apple ID) so the same Apple ID cannot immediately sign up again. It is removed if the ban is lifted.
  • To stop the free welcome coins from being collected again and again, we keep a separate one-way hash of each Apple ID that has received them. It is kept after the account is deleted, is not linked to any profile, and is used only so that a new account on the same Apple ID starts without the welcome coins.

g) Purchases

  • Purchases are made through Apple. We receive and verify a signed purchase record from Apple (transaction identifier, product, date). We never see your payment card details.

h) Technical data

  • Your IP address is used briefly to limit sign-ups and abuse (rate limiting) and to keep the service secure. These counters expire within minutes or hours.
  • Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes requests to run the service and may keep standard operational logs.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app currently contains no advertising or analytics software from third parties.

4. How we use your information

  • To run the game: sign-in, matchmaking, game rooms, coins, prizes, hints, levels, wins and tournaments.
  • To keep the community safe: filtering chat, handling reports, blocks, mutes and bans, and preventing cheating.
  • To keep the service working and secure, and to fix problems.
  • To follow the law and protect our rights.

5. Who we share it with

  • Other players see your username, portrait, level, win count, tournament results, and whatever you say in a room you are in.
  • Service providers that run Tawlah for us: Apple (sign-in and purchases) and Cloudflare (hosting and storage). They may only use the data to provide their service.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people’s safety.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, friends, blocks and coin data: until you delete your account.
  • Chat and emote log: 14 days.
  • Guest sessions: about 24 hours.
  • Moderation audit log: kept for accountability (it contains the username at that time), also after account deletion.
  • Purchase records: kept without your name or account link, as needed for accounting and refund handling, after account deletion.
  • Match history and tournament results that involve other players: kept so their history and the leaderboards stay correct, but your name is replaced by “Deleted player” after you delete your account.
  • Welcome-coins record (a one-way hash of your Apple ID, see 2f): kept after account deletion, not linked to a profile.

7. Deleting your account and your choices

  • In the app: Settings › Delete account. You must leave any game room first. We also ask Apple to revoke the app’s access to your Apple account.
  • What is deleted: your profile and sign-in, friends, requests, blocks, coin balance and coin records, daily bonus records, and the chat lines you wrote. Reports you made or that were made about you stay for moderation, no longer linked to a profile.
  • What stays: see section 6 (anonymised match history and tournament results, anonymised purchase records, the moderation audit log, the welcome-coins record).
  • You can change your portrait any time, block and report players, and mute emotes in Settings.
  • Depending on the laws where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us at the email above and we will answer within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Tawlah is rated 18+ on the App Store and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from people under 18. If you believe a young person has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Chat is filtered and every player can report, block and mute others.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Apple access tokens are stored encrypted. Coin changes are made only by our servers, never by the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Where your data is processed

Our providers operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own. We rely on our providers’ safeguards for such transfers.

11. Terms

Purchases are made through Apple and follow Apple’s terms. Use of the app is covered by Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

12. Changes

If we change this policy (for example if we add ads or push notifications), we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.

13. Contact

fastelkoo@gmail.com — for questions, data requests, or to report a problem. See also Support.
